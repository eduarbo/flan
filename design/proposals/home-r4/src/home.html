What’s under the frame

Low profile14.5 mm

Choc v1 hot-swap sockets under a flush magnetic frame.

Layout18 × 17 mm

Choc spacing, column stagger and three splayed thumb keys per half.

Wirelessnice!nano v2

nRF52840 running ZMK: Bluetooth LE, five host profiles, USB-C.

Displays2 × nice!view

Low-power memory LCD on each half, framed by the display cover.

Sizes36–54 keys

Four sizes: add the outer column, the number row or both. Same thumbs.

Battery100 mAh

1S LiPo per half, under the controller. 110 mAh pack also fits.

Looks28 · 6 · 4

Themes, magnetic display frames and case styles. Three KLP Lamé key shapes.

Open hardwareGPL-3.0

KiCad, FreeCAD, Gerbers, STEP, STL and 3MF. Print it, order it, change it.
