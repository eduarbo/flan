# Trademarks and official kits

Flan's design files are licensed under [GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE), with the third-party terms in [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md). Anyone may build, modify and sell them under those terms. The license covers the files, not the brand.

**Flan marks:** the names Flan, Flan36, Flan42, Flan46 and Flan54, the Flan wordmark and the tecla-flan mark in `docs/branding/` are trademarks of Eduardo Ruiz. As an additional term under GPL-3.0 section 7(e), the license grants no rights to use them.

## Allowed without asking

- Build, modify and use Flan for yourself, or give it away.
- Order PCBs from the published files for your own build, marks included, and give away or sell at cost the spare boards from that order.
- Share builds, photos, reviews, guides and add-ons that describe what they are, such as "my Flan36 build" or "a tenting kit for Flan36".
- Publish forks under a different name and logo, stating that they are based on Flan.

## Needs a license

- Selling PCBs, kits or assembled keyboards named Flan, or carrying a Flan mark on the board, case, packaging or listing.
- Describing a product as official, authorized or endorsed.

## Selling without the brand

The GPL already allows this. Remove the Flan wordmark and the tecla-flan mark from the silkscreen and case, use your own product name, and meet the license: keep the notices in [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md) and provide the corresponding source. Plain-text statements such as "based on Flan" or "compatible with Flan36" are fine when they do not look official.
