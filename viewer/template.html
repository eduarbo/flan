An original design

The keyboard and its CAD are designed by Eduardo Ruiz. It is inspired by Piantor by beekeeb and keeps its 36 key positions and angles; the PCBs, case, frames and firmware are original.

Keycap meshes and some component models come from credited third-party libraries.

Build status & dimensions

Routed digital prototype. PCB DRC, ERC and schematic parity checks pass. Physical assembly and hardware operation still need validation.
