# Flan logo

The selected logo: the **tecla-flan** mark, a low-profile keycap in three-quarter view with caramel dripping down the front, beside **flan** set in Fraunces.

![Flan logo](images/branding/flan-lockup.png)

**[View logo](https://eduarbo.github.io/flan/branding.html)** · **[Download SVG kit](https://eduarbo.github.io/flan/branding/flan/flan-logo-svg-kit.zip)** · [Usage guide](branding/flan/README.md)

The kit has the lockup and the mark, each in a display construction and a heavier silkscreen construction, plus the **colour mark** in custard and caramel, which is the site's favicon. All files are closed outlines in millimetres, with no font dependency. The silkscreen lockup is installed on the back of both revI boards with the edition lines and the maker's credit: `by`, the eduarbo cube and `eduarbo`. The cube comes from [eduarbo.com](https://eduarbo.com) (`public/favicon.svg`); in one ink, three thin cuts keep its faces apart (`tools/branding/eduarbo.py`).

The name family is **Flan + key count**: Flan 36 today; 42 adds an outer pinky column, 46 a number row, 54 both. The design study is in [naming R1–R4](../design/proposals/naming-r4/README.md).

The previous logo, Flan36 Outline, stays in [`docs/branding/outline/`](branding/outline/README.md) as history.

The project's canonical repository is **[eduarbo/flan](https://github.com/eduarbo/flan)**.
