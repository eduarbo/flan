**EN · “Looks expensive / complicated”:** “It is a DIY project and assembly does require tools and soldering. The parts list lets you judge whether it fits your project; I’m not presenting it as a ready-to-use purchase.”

**EN · “It is just a render”:** “Yes, for now. I’ve ordered the parts and this shows the design I’m going to test. The next step is documenting the real build, including anything that fails.”
